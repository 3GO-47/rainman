RAINMAN brand

Three themes, one set of variables. Every page reads them off html[data-theme] and remembers the choice in localStorage["rm-theme"]. Dark is the site as it was before themes existed and does not change — light and rain are built by remapping, never by editing dark.

Source of truth: scripts/brand.py · written up in notes/brand.md · original sheet in notes/brand/RAINMAN_Brand.pdf.
