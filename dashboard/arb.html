How the Arb Engine prices a bet

Sources

Every main line (moneyline, spread, total) and main player prop at DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers and ESPN BET through The Odds API, plus the Kalshi and Polymarket exchanges (YES at the ask, the other side at 1 − bid; Kalshi's 7% × P × (1−P) taker fee is added to the cost). Started games are dropped — live prices are not a market we price.

Fair probability

Each source that quotes both sides gives one fair: books are de-vigged proportionally, exchanges use the mid-point (weighted 1.5×). The fair for a price at book X is the consensus of every source except X — a book cannot vouch for its own number. Anytime TD is quoted YES-only, so each team's YES prices are scaled to the team's expected number of distinct scorers (season average from the game logs × team implied total); anything under 10% is shown but never called an edge.

Edge and stake

Edge = fair × decimal price − 1. Stake = ¼ Kelly of the bankroll in the header. The RAINMAN prop model (NFL, players with real usage) is shown beside the market as an independent opinion, never mixed into the fair.

Confidence A / B / C

0–100 from: how many two-sided sources set the fair (1 → 30, 2 → 52, 3 → 66, 4 → 76, 5+ → 84), minus how much they disagree (−1 per point of spread in their fairs), +8 when an exchange is among them, ±10 for the consensus moving toward or away from the bet since the opener, −10/−25 when the edge is over 8% / 15% (usually a stale or limited line), capped at 60 for YES-only markets. A ≥ 70, B ≥ 50.

Steam and CLV

Every Odds API pull saves one consensus snapshot per market. STEAM shows the opener → latest path. CLV scores each saved play against the latest (or closing) consensus for its side: positive means you beat the market's final number — the only durable evidence an edge is real.
