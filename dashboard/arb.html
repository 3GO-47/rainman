Every line, every source, one number.The same bet priced at every book we can reach — DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and more through The Odds API — beside the Kalshi and Polymarket exchanges. Each side gets a fair probability from the consensus of all sources (exchange mid-points, de-vigged book prices), so every price turns into an expected value. When the two sides of one market pay out more than 100% combined across sources, that's an arbitrage and the stake split is shown. Exchange fees are already in the prices.

How to read it — gold chip = the best price for that side · the % on a chip = expected value at that price (green = you are paid more than fair, red = less) · fair = the consensus probability · click any chip to save it as a play.
